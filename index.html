The honest caveat — this very page

Wraps are the leak: a public deposit reveals an amount entering the pool — but not whose note it becomes. Spent nullifiers reveal that a note died — but not which one. Timing analysis can narrow linkages (this page's activity chart is exactly the kind of data such analysis would use), yet no note-to-note link is published anywhere. Amounts in the points leaderboard and wrap feed are edge disclosures, not pool balances. The bridge is the most linkable part of the system: burns and mints are matched publicly by the protocol itself.
